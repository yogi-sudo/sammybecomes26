Field notes

Happy birthday, Mero puntu

Words Palbika · Photographs six years of camera roll

Honestly, I don't even know how we got here, but I'm really glad we did. Out of all the people we met in college, I got you — either luck, or a clerical error nobody has bothered to correct.

From seven people in a group to just two of us still going strong. I think we did pretty well, considering neither of us has arrived anywhere on time since 2020.

Here's to more café plans, stupid decisions, long talks, unnecessary gossip, career discussions, trips, prayers and somehow still being idiots together.

Matching without planning it. Allegedly.

“You stopped feeling like just a best friend. You became family.”
